Empire Updates

President Rule: The President is the founder of Classic Empire Ltd and is the person who established this Empire.

The President is the permanent President of Classic Empire Ltd and the position of President will never change under the normal system.

The President can personally appoint or transfer the position of President to another person only by the President's own decision.

No other person or authority can change the President.

Examination System

Applicants who are approved for an examination will receive a unique Exam Code.

The applicant must enter the Exam Code and select the correct post in the Examination Portal.

Each examination contains:

  • 18 Objective Questions
  • 2 Subjective Questions
  • Objective qualifying level: 60%
  • Subjective answers are saved for manual checking

About Classic Empire Ltd

Classic Empire Ltd is a fictional school-style administration and examination project.

The project contains its own administrative structure, currency system, salary system, application system and examination portal.

The examination system is designed for applicants applying for available Classic Empire Ltd posts.
