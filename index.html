About Classic Empire Ltd

The President is the founder of Classic Empire Ltd and is the person who established this Empire. The President is the permanent President of Classic Empire Ltd and the position of President will never change under the normal system.

The President can personally appoint or transfer the position of President to another person only by the President's own decision. No other person or authority can change the President.
