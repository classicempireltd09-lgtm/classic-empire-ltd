New Empire Changes
01. President System: The President is the founder and permanent President under the normal system.
02. Administration: The current administrative structure uses President, President's Assistant, DM, DM's Assistant, SDM, SP and DSP posts.
03. Removed Posts: PM, Ministers, Management/Founder/Chairman posts, CID, Bodyguard and Election Department are not part of the current system.
04. Salary: Salaries are official weekly salaries and are paid every Thursday.
05. Currency: Classic Dinar (CD/CLD) is the fictional official currency of the Empire.
06. Applications: Eligible people can apply for available posts using the application form below. The President post is not available through the normal application form.

About Classic Empire Ltd

The President is the founder of Classic Empire Ltd and is the person who established this Empire. The President is the permanent President of Classic Empire Ltd and the position of President will never change under the normal system.


The President can personally appoint or transfer the position of President to another person only by the President's own decision. No other person or authority can change the President.
