🏛️ Administration Structure

The Classic Empire Ltd administration is organized around the President and the administrative and police posts.

The President is at the top of the Classic Empire Ltd system, followed by the President's Assistant and the administrative structure.

👑 Permanent President Rule

The President is the founder of Classic Empire Ltd and is the person who established this Empire.

The President is the permanent President of Classic Empire Ltd and the position of President will never change under the normal system.

The President can personally appoint or transfer the position of President to another person only by the President's own decision. No other person or authority can change the President.
