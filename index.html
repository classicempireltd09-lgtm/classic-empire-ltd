ABOUT US

Built on ideas. Driven by teamwork.

Classic Empire Ltd. is a fictional company for an educational game. It gives players a simple way to experience leadership, teamwork, departments and business decisions.

About the Creator: I created this game for my friends as a fun way to learn, work together and enjoy a shared game world.

Our Story

We turn ideas into plans, plans into action, and teamwork into results. Every role has a purpose, and every member can contribute.

“Dream • Plan • Build • Grow”

What we focus on

Leadership, responsible decision-making, creativity, communication and learning through play.

Important: Classic Empire Ltd. is a fictional/game company and is not presented as a real registered business.
