Permanent President Rule

The President is the founder of Classic Empire Ltd and is the person who established this Empire.

The President is the permanent President of Classic Empire Ltd and the position of President will never change under the normal system.

The President can personally appoint or transfer the position of President to another person only by the President's own decision.

No other person or authority can change the President.

Application System

Applicants can submit applications through the official application form. Approved applicants receive an exam code through the system.

Examination System

The Classic Empire examination system contains objective and subjective questions, a 30-minute exam session and server-controlled exam access.
